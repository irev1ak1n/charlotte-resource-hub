After-School STEM Signups Open

Youth Programs South End

After-School programs are available at Charlotte Mecklenburg Schools (CMS) to help students understand a certain topic better or go into clubs to help understand what they are interested in. These are there to help the student understand more about a topic they are interested in or needing help with.

The After-School Programs will start immediately after school and end 2 hours later.
