Help Your Community

Small actions can make a real difference. Volunteering your time, skills, or energy helps support people and organizations working to improve life across Charlotte.

Donate With Purpose

Donations help provide essential support to families in need. Giving items, funds, or supplies allows trusted local organizations to continue their work in the community.

Share Trusted Opportunities

If you know of a nonprofit, program, or service doing meaningful work, you can share it with us. Each suggestion is reviewed before being added to the hub.
