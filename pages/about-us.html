About Charlotte Resource Hub

The Charlotte Resource Hub connects the Queen City community with trusted local support. We centralize information on housing, health, education, crisis support, jobs, and other essential services so people can find help quickly and confidently.

This project began with a simple observation. Help exists all around Charlotte, but it is often hard to find when you need it most. Information is spread across many different websites, social media pages, and flyers, which makes it easy to miss a program that could make a real difference.

As students in the Charlotte area, we saw how this impacts families, teens, and individuals trying to navigate resources. Many organizations are doing incredible work, but there was not one clear place that brought everything together in a way that felt organized and easy to use.

Our purpose is to build one accessible hub where community members can discover reliable local resources in one place. We review submissions, organize services by category, and design the site to work smoothly on any device. The goal is clarity, not complexity.

Charlotte Resource Hub is a tool built to strengthen connections across our community and make support easier to reach for anyone who needs it.
