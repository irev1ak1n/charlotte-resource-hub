Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: February 2026

Using the Directory "City Resources"

How do I search for resources?
Use the search bar and category filters on the Directory page to quickly narrow down resources by topic (housing, food assistance, healthcare, and more).
How do I find resources near me?
Many listings include addresses, service areas, and contact links. If a listing looks close, check the organization’s website or call to confirm eligibility and location details.
What if a resource listing is outdated?
Details can change (hours, requirements, phone numbers). If you notice something outdated, please submit an update through the Contact Us page so we can review and correct it.

Resources & Verification

Are all resources officially verified?
We aim to include trusted organizations and accurate information using credible sources. However, we recommend verifying details directly with the organization before visiting or applying.
Do you guarantee services or availability?
No. Organizations may have eligibility rules, limited capacity, or changing hours. Charlotte Resource Hub provides informational listings only.
How often is the directory updated?
Updates are made as we review new submissions and check listings. If you notice changes, sending an update helps us improve accuracy faster.

Submitting a Resource / Getting Involved

How do I submit a new resource or volunteer opportunity?
Use the Contact Us (or Get Involved) form to submit the organization name, what they provide, and a reliable link or phone number. We review submissions before adding them.
What should I include in my submission?
Include: organization name, service type, address/service area, phone/email, website link, and any key eligibility details (who qualifies and what’s required).
How long does it take for a submission to appear?
Timing depends on verification and available team time. If your submission includes a clear link or phone number, review is faster.

Safety & Emergencies

Is this website for emergencies?
No. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. For urgent mental health support, contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US).
What if I need help right now?
If it’s urgent, use emergency services. Otherwise, contact organizations directly using the phone numbers or websites in the directory—many can provide the fastest next steps.

Newsletter & Privacy

What will you email me about?
Newsletter emails may include community highlights, resource updates, and local opportunities related to the hub’s topics.
How do I unsubscribe?
Every email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. You can opt out at any time.
Do you sell my data?
No. We do not sell personal information. See the Privacy Policy page for more details on how form submissions are handled.

About This Project

Who runs Charlotte Resource Hub?
Charlotte Resource Hub is a student-created project for the Technology Student Association (TSA) Webmaster theme. It’s designed to help residents discover helpful community resources.
How can I contact the team?
Use the Contact Us page to reach us. If you’re submitting a resource, including a link or phone number helps us verify it more quickly.
