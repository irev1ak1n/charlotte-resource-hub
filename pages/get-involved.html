Suggest a Community Resource

Help us keep Charlotte connected by strengthening our community resource directory. If you know a nonprofit organization, local program, clinic, shelter, hotline, training opportunity, or any service that supports people in the Charlotte area, you can submit it here for consideration.

Every submission is carefully reviewed by our team to ensure accuracy, relevance, and safety before it is added to the public directory. This helps keep the information reliable and useful for everyone who depends on these resources.

Please include a working website or phone number so community members can easily contact the resource and get help when they need it.

Submissions are reviewed before publication to prevent spam and ensure accuracy.
